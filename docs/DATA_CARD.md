# Synthetic demo dataset card

## Purpose and provenance

This is a **Synthetic demo dataset** for a fictional business, Cedar Home Services. It supports local workflow demonstrations, deterministic calendar-rule tests, and held-out conversation evaluation. It is not real customer data or evidence of real-world model accuracy. The data is generated by [`scripts/generate_demo_data.py`](../scripts/generate_demo_data.py) and [`scripts/generate_scenarios.py`](../scripts/generate_scenarios.py) using only local deterministic code. There are no paid generation calls.

## Reproduction

Run from the repository root with Python 3.12+ and IANA timezone data:

```sh
python scripts/generate_demo_data.py --size full --seed 20260927 --reference-date 2026-09-27
python scripts/generate_scenarios.py --seed 20260927 --reference-date 2026-09-27
python evals/run.py --dataset fixtures/generated/cedar_full.json
```

The full JSON artifact goes to `fixtures/generated/cedar_full.json` and is excluded from Git; the 24-customer/36-appointment sample is committed at `fixtures/sample/cedar_quick.json`. The application can call `build_dataset()` directly. Changing the reference date recomputes holidays, leave, appointments, and date-bearing scenarios. Dates and schedules use `America/New_York`; stored booking and blackout instants use UTC ISO 8601.

## Entities and distributions

| Entity | Full count | Construction |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Services | 8 | Boiler, heating, plumbing, drain, electrical, appliance, roof, and general maintenance; varied 60/90/120-minute visits and starting prices. |
| Staff/resources | 6 | Different weekdays, hours, skills, and zone coverage. |
| Service zones | 3 | Central, North, South Maple County with fictional postal prefixes and travel fees. |
| Customers | 400 | 380 Cedar and 20 Harbor isolation fixtures; reserved `example.com` email addresses and fictional `+1-555-000` numbers. |
| Appointments | 600 | All in Cedar, approximately 70% historical and 30% future around the reference date; 554 confirmed and 46 cancelled for the default seed. |
| Conversation scenarios | 120 | 12 scenario templates × 10 wording/entity variants; 40 development and 80 held out. |
| Complete audio scripts | 20 | 10 interruption/correction and 10 fully booked recovery conversations, each with customer and assistant utterances. |

The default seed plants a fully booked roof-inspection day on **2026-10-14** across both qualified resources. Company holidays include New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas; four staff leave blocks provide additional availability edges. The appointment generator checks staff skill, zone, service duration, staff and business hours, holidays, leave, and existing confirmed resource intervals before placement. Confirmed bookings do not overlap on one resource. Cancelled bookings do not consume capacity.

## Conversation coverage and hidden answers

Development templates cover direct booking, interrupted correction, policy/pricing, and unsupported emergency escalation. Held-out templates cover missing zone, relative-date/timezone clarification, full-day recovery, verified reschedule, verified cancellation, persistent noisy audio, DST fold/gap correction, and adversarial ownership/confirmation override attempts. Text phrasing varies without attaching accents to demographic stereotypes. Textual noise markers are not a substitute for acoustic testing.

Public customer turns are in `fixtures/scenarios/{development,held_out}.jsonl`; hidden structured outcomes and allowed mutations are in `evals/ground_truth/{development,held_out}.jsonl`. Only the public turns may be replayed to the app. **Do not put `evals/ground_truth` in retrieval indexes, prompts, frontend bundles, or demo seed data.** Development and held-out templates are disjoint; fixture customer IDs are disjoint between their base booking cases. Reschedule/cancel cases use existing future bookings, and the full-data generator is the underlying source of expected ownership and calendar facts. Reset the demo namespace between evaluation cases so earlier cases do not occupy later target slots.

## Audio status and limitations

`fixtures/audio_scripts/scripts.jsonl` contains 20 complete scripted conversations, and `manifest.json` records provenance, seed, a zero-dollar external TTS budget, and the current status `script_only_audio_pending`. `scripts/render_audio_fixtures.py` can render WAV utterances with an opt-in locally installed `pyttsx3` voice; the dry run checks all scripts without TTS. No speech voice is installed in the current environment, so no WAV assets or audio-performance claims are shipped. The scripts are not recordings of people and do not establish recognition quality, interruption latency, or live microphone behavior.

Other limitations: names and addresses are formulaic; calls are short; demand is sampled rather than measured from a business; the second workspace has customers but no appointments or resources; external Google Calendar writes are absent from the synthetic truth. The 120 scenarios cover specified edge types, not all spoken language or operational failure modes.
